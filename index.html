Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about playing and ranking.

Are the K-drama quizzes completely free to play? +

Yes! All of our Korean drama trivia quizzes are 100% free. You can play as a guest, but we recommend creating a free account so your scores are saved to the global leaderboards.

How are the leaderboard scores calculated? +

Your final score is a combination of accurate answers and speed. Each question has a timer. The faster you select the correct answer, the more bonus points you earn. Incorrect answers yield zero points.

Do you add new quizzes regularly? +

Absolutely. We update our database weekly with quizzes on both timeless classics and currently airing trending dramas on Netflix, Viki, and other streaming platforms.
